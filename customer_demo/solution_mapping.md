# Solution mapping

Fictional discovery-to-demo mapping. Benefits are expected opportunities, not measured client outcomes.

| Customer pain point | Business requirement | Solution/demo feature | Expected business benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manual intercompany reconciliation across spreadsheets | Match reciprocal activity and identify differences | Service/balance pairing, EUR differences and source reconciliation | Earlier identification of discrepancies and less repetitive checking |
| Repeated manual GBP-to-EUR conversion | Apply approved rates consistently | Rate table and local/rate/EUR audit trail | More consistent conversion and easier review |
| Excel-based consolidation adjustments are difficult to trace | Explain and balance every adjustment | Journal IDs, explanations, debit/credit totals and controls | Clearer adjustment review and fewer unexplained postings |
| Consolidated reporting requires copying many files | Produce repeatable group statements | Python-generated consolidated P&L, balance sheet and dashboard | Faster report preparation with consistent outputs |
| Minority interests are calculated separately | Make ownership allocations understandable | Germany 80% parent/20% NCI profit and equity allocation | Easier explanation of group versus parent results |
| Close checks depend on personal checklists | Make essential controls visible | Five Consolidation Controls dashboard checks | More repeatable review and clearer exception handling |
