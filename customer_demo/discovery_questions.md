# Discovery questions

Fictional discovery exercise for a French finance team; not a record of client work.

1. How many legal entities are included in your consolidation, and who owns the scope?
2. Which accounting standards and local-to-group account mappings do you use?
3. What is your group reporting currency? Which foreign currencies do you manage?
4. Who approves FX rates, and which rate types are required?
5. How do you currently reconcile intercompany invoices and outstanding balances?
6. Which consolidation activities and adjustments are still performed manually in Excel?
7. How long does your monthly or quarterly close take?
8. What are the main pain points, recurring errors and delays during close?
9. How are ownership changes, acquisitions and minority interests managed?
10. Which reporting outputs, drill-downs and management views are required?
11. Who submits, reviews and approves entity reporting packages?
12. Which controls are performed manually, and what evidence must be retained?
13. Which source systems, transaction volumes and historical periods must be integrated?
14. What measurable outcome would make a pilot successful?
