# Practical presales demonstration

This is a fictional portfolio presentation for a French finance team. Allow approximately 15 minutes, followed by discussion. Do not present it as previous client work or a compliant statutory product.

1. **Understand the customer's current process.** Ask about scope, currencies, close duration and manual reconciliation. Confirm which problems a pilot should measure.
2. **Show entity and financial data.** Open Entities and Trial Balances. Explain France, Germany and UK, debit/credit signs, annual scope and Germany's 80% ownership.
3. **Show FX translation.** On FX Rates and Trial Balances, follow UK revenue from £800,000 through 1.20 to €960,000. Explain that equal opening/average/closing rates intentionally avoid a translation reserve in V1.
4. **Show intercompany reconciliation.** Follow France's €500,000 service invoice and Germany's expense, then the €200,000 unpaid balance. Explain that these are related views of one invoice. Show reciprocal matching and zero EUR differences. A mismatched input stops the pipeline.
5. **Show consolidation adjustments.** Review the service, balance and investment journals. Explain why internal revenue and expenses disappear while group profit stays unchanged. Show balanced debits and credits and opening NCI recognition.
6. **Show consolidated statements.** Present €4.46m revenue, €620k profit, €4.04m assets and €2.82m equity. Explain €40k minority profit and €240k closing minority equity. Confirm assets equal €1.22m liabilities plus €2.82m equity.
7. **Show management dashboard.** Review the KPIs, five controls and consolidation flow. Explain adjustment volume versus profit impact and how to refresh the report from CSV inputs.
8. **Discuss customer questions and next steps.** Compare the demo to actual requirements. Capture gaps around varying FX, acquisition accounting, source integration, security and approvals. Agree pilot acceptance criteria before proposing an expanded scope.
